Clear Names, Honest Boundaries

Power is a story, not one magic number.

Battakorey keeps advertised capability, negotiated contracts, measured input, battery flow, and component estimates separate. Missing or implausible private readings simply disappear instead of pretending to be exact.

Quiet by Design

Your battery data stays on your Mac.

01

No account. No analytics.

Battakorey reads local power information and does not collect or send battery data.

02

No root access.

Extra telemetry does not require disabling System Integrity Protection or running as root.

03

Graceful when unavailable.

Private AppleSMC and IOReport readings vary by Mac. Unsupported channels stay out of sight.

Battakorey is intentionally not sandboxed so it can read additional local telemetry. It is distributed directly, not through the Mac App Store. The download is signed and notarized.
